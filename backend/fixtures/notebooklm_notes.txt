Strategy briefing from a premium selling video on NIFTY options.

Entry rules discussed:
- Sell defined-risk premium when IV rank is above 40.
- Prefer 7 to 21 days to expiry (DTE).
- Use iron condor or credit spread with width around 100 points.

Exit rules:
- Take profit near 50% of maximum credit.
- Cut loss if loss reaches 100% of credit received.

Risk:
- Keep size small, max 2 lots for retail.
- Do not trade if daily loss exceeds 2% of capital.
