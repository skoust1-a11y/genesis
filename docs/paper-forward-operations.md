## Input and cost evidence

Index candles come from the existing Yahoo scanner feed. Upstox's allowlisted contract API establishes instrument identity and lot size. Its V3 full WebSocket feed supplies book and delta snapshots. Only full live messages from an open derivatives market with upstream `currentTs` within ten seconds are accepted. Initial snapshots, last-trade timestamps, partial messages, wrong contracts, stale/future timestamps, invalid signs and inverted books cannot authorize a simulated fill. This observes the full tick snapshot; it does not claim a separately timestamped exchange Greek calculation.

The configured RSS feeds are refreshed every five minutes, sanitized, persisted in the market store and passed to the existing news guard. Missing or stale recent headlines block new entries while exit monitoring continues. Keyword-based news gating is not a complete macro-event calendar.

Each session must obtain and save a validated Upstox tariff page for that exact date. Published rates are checked against the supported schedule. Its raw evidence, hash and date are persisted in `logs/paper-forward/fees`. A new immutable daily fee policy is then used for paper accrual. Changed/missing tariff evidence blocks fills. Existing historical policies and persisted historical charges are not rewritten.

## Versioned protective paper exits

The `fresh-bid-v1` paper exit policy separates liquidation from entry selection. New entries still require the configured maximum spread and the same V2 risk, lot, delta, capital and fee checks. A protective partial/final exit may sell the exact held contract at its fresh bid, minus the existing adverse tick with the minimum-tick floor, even when its percentage spread exceeds the entry cap. It requires an open market, an unexpired identified contract, a fresh positive noninverted two-sided book and a bid of at least INR 0.05. Missing/stale/future quotes, wrong held identity, absent fees or invalid books cannot produce a fill. Their exposure remains unresolved. This policy does not claim executable depth for historical modeled books.

`PAPER_EXIT_QUOTE_POLICY` defaults to `entry-spread-v1` for compatibility. Comparison runs select and pin the common exit policy and its source identity in their manifest. Baseline and candidate journals bind that policy immutably; changing it requires separate journals, and an unbound trading history cannot be assigned the new policy retroactively. SELL receipts carry `paper-exit:fresh-bid-v1`; BUY receipts retain the entry policy. Old trades and previously recorded fees are not rewritten.

## Candidate peak-net-profit giveback

The owner clarified that giveback is a percentage of peak profit, not the full option premium: bought at 100, peak at 150, a 20% profit giveback corresponds to a 140 trigger before costs. For paper accounting the exact basis is current bid liquidation value, after adverse tick slippage and estimated exit charges, less the persisted entry debit and charges. Only fresh, post-entry quotes for the held contract can raise the recorded peak. Hypothetical liquidation marks never change cash or create fills.

The candidate variant arms its trail once peak net profit reaches 1R of the persisted initial estimated monetary stop risk. The threshold is 80% of the running peak for the selected 20% giveback, rounded conservatively to paisa. The fixed 3R take-profit is removed; the 3R index level remains a reference in the stored entry. Initial underlying stop, 15:00 entry cutoff, 15:10 square-off, one position, one attempt per zone, two-step OTM selection and all monetary/quote gates remain unchanged. Baseline entry/exit strategy rules remain the comparison control.

The quote-only profit monitor runs independently at a one-second cadence during active supervision, using the local Upstox book cache without fetching candles. New admissions are blocked if this monitor throws an unexpected error; main-loop exit checks continue. SQLite transactions serialize it with entry/exit processing and prevent duplicate sales. It is stopped and joined before the candidate journal lock is released. Neither one-second sampling nor a trailing trigger guarantees a particular realized giveback during gaps, stale data or lack of executable quotes.
